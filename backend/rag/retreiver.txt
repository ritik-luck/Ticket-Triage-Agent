File: rag/retreiver.py

Overview:
This module performs semantic search over the ChromaDB vector collection. It takes a ticket query, turns it into an embedding, searches for the closest knowledge-base chunks, and then converts raw vector distances into a more readable similarity score.

The reason this module exists is to bridge retrieval and reasoning. The rest of the system needs a simple answer to one question: what internal knowledge is most relevant to this ticket?

Classes and methods:
- Retriever.__init__(top_k=3): Sets how many nearby matches should be returned for one query.
- Retriever.retrieve(query): Validates the query, generates an embedding, searches the collection, converts the raw distances into scores, and returns the best-matching chunks plus the top score.
- Retriever._convert_distance_to_score(distance): Converts a distance value into a normalized similarity score that is easier for the rest of the pipeline to interpret.

Instances:
- retriever: Shared retriever used by the augmentation manager.
